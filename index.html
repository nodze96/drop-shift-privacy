Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Drop Shift.

Game: Drop Shift Developer: N96S Last updated: August 6, 2026

1. Overview

N96S built Drop Shift as a mobile game. We respect your privacy and aim to clearly explain what information may be processed when you play the game.

N96S does not directly require you to create an account and does not directly collect your name, postal address, phone number, or email address through the game. However, third-party services integrated into the game may automatically collect certain information as described below.

2. Information That May Be Collected

When advertising or platform services are active, the following categories of information may be processed by third-party providers:

  • Device or other identifiers, including advertising identifiers where permitted.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address.
  • Device information, operating system, language, app version, and connection information.
  • Advertising interactions, such as ad impressions, views, clicks, and completed rewarded ads.
  • App diagnostics and technical information used to maintain service reliability and prevent fraud.
  • Gameplay-related platform information, such as achievements, leaderboard scores, or player identifiers, if Google Play Games features are enabled.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Displaying contextual or personalized advertisements where legally permitted.
  • Measuring advertising performance and limiting repeated advertisements.
  • Providing rewarded advertisements and verifying that an advertisement was completed.
  • Detecting fraud, abuse, security incidents, and technical problems.
  • Providing optional Google Play Games features such as achievements or leaderboards.
  • Complying with legal obligations and user privacy choices.

4. Unity Ads

Drop Shift uses or may use Unity Ads to display advertisements. Unity and its advertising partners may collect and process device identifiers, advertising interaction data, app diagnostics, and related technical information for advertising, analytics, security, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

Depending on your region, device settings, age-related settings, and consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Unity may present its own privacy consent interface where required.

Learn more in Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and review Unity's privacy choices.

5. Google Play Games Services

If enabled in Drop Shift, Google Play Games Services may process a player identifier, display name, profile information, achievements, leaderboard scores, and game progress to provide platform features requested by the player.

Google's handling of information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Sharing of Information

N96S does not sell personal information directly. Information may be processed or shared by Unity, Google, advertising partners, service providers, and authorities where required by law. These providers process information under their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

7. Data Retention and Security

N96S does not maintain a separate database of player personal information for Drop Shift. Third-party providers retain information according to their own policies and legal requirements. We use reasonable measures to protect information under our control, but no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Privacy Choices and Data Deletion

You may be able to control advertising personalization through your device settings, Google account settings, consent screens shown in the game, or Unity's privacy tools.

Drop Shift does not create a separate N96S user account. To ask whether N96S holds any information about you, or to request deletion of information under our control, contact us using the email address below. Requests concerning data controlled by Unity or Google may also need to be submitted directly to those providers.

9. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly to N96S. We aim to use age-appropriate privacy and advertising settings where required by applicable law and platform rules. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, integrated services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page and the “Last updated” date will be revised.
