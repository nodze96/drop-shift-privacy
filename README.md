# Privacy Policy for Drop Shift

**Last updated: August 6, 2026**

## 1. Overview



N96S built Drop Shift as a mobile game.

We respect your privacy and aim to clearly explain what information may be processed when you use the game.

N96S does not require users to create a separate account and does not directly collect names, postal addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses through the game.

However, third-party services integrated into Drop Shift may automatically collect certain information.

## 2. Information That May Be Collected

Third-party services may process the following information:

- Device identifiers and advertising identifiers, where permitted
- IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address
- Device model, operating system, language, and app version
- Internet connection and technical information
- Advertisement impressions, views, clicks, and completed rewarded advertisements
- App diagnostics, crash information, and performance data
- Gameplay information, achievements, leaderboard scores, or player identifiers if Google Play Games Services are enabled

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used for:

- Displaying advertisements
- Providing personalized or non-personalized advertisements where permitted
- Measuring advertisement performance
- Limiting how often the same advertisement is shown
- Providing rewards after completed rewarded advertisements
- Detecting fraud, abuse, and security incidents
- Fixing technical problems
- Providing achievements and leaderboard features
- Complying with legal obligations and privacy choices

## 4. Unity Ads

Drop Shift uses or may use **Unity Ads** to display advertisements.

Unity and its advertising partners may process information such as:

- Device identifiers
- Advertising identifiers
- Advertisement interaction data
- App diagnostics
- Technical information
- Information used for security and fraud prevention

Depending on the user's region, age-related settings, device settings, and consent choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Unity may display a privacy consent screen where required.

Unity Privacy Policy:

https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Unity Privacy Choices:

https://unity.com/legal/do-not-sell-my-personal-information

## 5. Google Play Games Services

If Google Play Games Services are enabled in Drop Shift, Google may process:

- Player identifiers
- Display names
- Profile information
- Achievements
- Leaderboard scores
- Game progress

This information is used to provide Google Play Games features.

Google Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Sharing of Information

N96S does not directly sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by Unity, Google, advertising partners, service providers, or authorities where required by law.

These third-party providers process information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

## 7. Data Retention and Security

N96S does not maintain a separate database containing personal information about Drop Shift players.

Third-party providers retain information according to their own privacy policies and legal requirements.

Reasonable measures are used to protect information, but no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Privacy Choices and Data Deletion

Users may be able to manage advertising personalization through:

- Device settings
- Google Account settings
- Privacy consent screens displayed in the game
- Unity privacy tools

Drop Shift does not create a separate N96S user account.

To ask whether N96S holds information about you or to request deletion of information under our control, contact us by email.

Requests concerning information controlled by Unity or Google may need to be sent directly to those providers.

## 9. Children's Privacy

N96S does not knowingly request children to directly provide personal information.

We aim to use age-appropriate advertising and privacy settings where required by applicable laws and Google Play policies.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game, integrated services, or legal requirements change.

Updates will be published on this page, and the date at the top of the policy will be revised.
